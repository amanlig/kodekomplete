## End-of-day handoff — signing key blocks Play release

The owner's Play Console screenshot shows that the corrected package AAB was
rejected because its signing certificate does not match the certificate required
by the existing Play app. A successful EAS build and signing-block check do not
establish Play signing compatibility. The newly generated EAS key was not checked
against the existing Play upload certificate before building.

- Play expects SHA-1: `5E:11:B4:4C:FD:D6:50:82:9A:CE:98:25:14:04:D6:32:B5:53:25:C4`.
- Uploaded AAB SHA-1: `A8:6B:0C:9D:8D:7D:53:34:A8:BB:5D:5B:A3:47:AF:35:84:A8:7F:29`.

No internal-testing release or physical installation has been completed. Keep
the corrected artifact as build evidence; uploading it again unchanged will not
resolve this mismatch. No signing credentials were replaced after the rejection,
and no upload-key reset was requested. Work is paused at the owner's request.
